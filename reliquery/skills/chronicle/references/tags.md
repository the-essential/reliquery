# Tag Vocabulary

Controlled vocabulary for the `tags` frontmatter field on character relics. Chronicle must draw from this list when applying tags. Any proposed new tag requires explicit author approval before committing — flag it in the Phase 4 outline and do not add it to this file without permission.

Tags fall into two categories: **psychological traits** (what drives or defines a character internally) and **narrative function** (what role they play in the story's machinery). A character typically gets 2–4 tags — enough to be meaningful, not so many the vocabulary loses signal.

---

## Psychological Traits

**`identity-fractured`**
A person whose sense of self has been destabilized, stolen, or overwritten. Applies to characters who don't know who they are, have had their history erased, or are living under a fabricated identity.
*Current: Eugene Isaacs, Justinian Floris (suspected)*

**`guilt-driven`**
Motivated primarily by guilt or a felt responsibility for harm done to others. Their choices circle back to something they can't undo.
*Current: Felix Dèstau*

**`loyalty-driven`**
Defined by personal loyalty that overrides self-interest. Would sacrifice their own safety or advantage to protect the person or principle they're committed to.
*Current: Eugene Isaacs, Felix Dèstau*

**`paranoid`**
Operates from deep distrust of systems, institutions, or people. Sees patterns others dismiss. Often turns out to be correct.
*Current: Felix Dèstau*

**`humor-as-armor`**
Uses wit, levity, or self-deprecation to manage vulnerability. The joke is load-bearing — remove it and the emotional structure collapses.
*Current: Eugene Isaacs*

**`calculating`**
Emotions are subordinate to strategic thinking. Acts from long-term interest even when warmth is on the surface.
*Current: Vic Chamberlain, Anastasia, Leonce Guillory*

**`guarded`**
Withholds personal history and trusts no one fully. Their past is a closed door, and they intend to keep it that way.
*Current: Anastasia, Leonce Guillory*

**`pragmatist`**
Adapts to circumstances without strong ideological commitment. Does what works. Not cynical — just unsentimental about method.
*Current: Vic Chamberlain, Kellon Geralt*

## Narrative Function

**`arc-catalyst`**
Their action or revelation sets the plot in motion or triggers a major escalation. The story pivots on something they do.
*Current: Eugene Isaacs, Gideon Marbòla*

**`mirror`**
Structurally parallels another character's arc or condition — same situation, different angle. Reading them beside their mirror clarifies both.
*Current: Justinian Floris (mirrors Gene's adjusted status), Chase Hopkins (mirrors Gene's dream-child)*

**`moral-anchor`**
Grounds the story's ethical stakes in human terms. Their distress or relief signals what the story thinks matters.
*Current: Felix Dèstau*

**`truth-seeker`**
Actively investigating what others want hidden. Driven by the need to understand, not just survive.
*Current: Felix Dèstau, Kellon Geralt*

**`unresolved-connection`**
Their link to another character is deliberately withheld from the reader. The nature of the connection is a live narrative question.
*Current: Clara Belle Hopkins, Anastasia, Justinian Floris*

**`institutional-face`**
Represents or embodies an organization's public image. What they say and do carries institutional weight beyond their personal stakes.
*Current: Vic Chamberlain, Clara Belle Hopkins*

**`offscreen-threat`**
Present through consequences only — felt in the damage they've caused, not in direct scene appearances.
*Current: Leonce Guillory*
