## Description

Sera moves through crowded rooms the way a scalpel moves through tissue — with economy and intent. She is slight enough to be overlooked and sharp enough to make that a fatal mistake. Dark hair cut short, practical. Eyes that catalogue exits before they register faces.

## Background

Recruited into the Iron Council at seventeen after the Thorngate Incident left her without family or illusions about the world's fairness. Miren Cade saw potential in the wreckage and offered structure in exchange for loyalty. Sera accepted because the alternative was nothing.

Twelve years of fieldwork turned her into one of the Council's most effective operatives — not the strongest or the most connected, but the most adaptable. She reads situations the way others read weather, adjusting before the storm arrives.

## Motivations

Sera wants control. Not power — control. The difference matters to her. Power is what other people have over you. Control is what you maintain over yourself. Every operation she runs, every relationship she cultivates, serves that distinction.

## Current Situation

Embedded in Duskhollow under diplomatic cover during the succession crisis. Her official mission is intelligence gathering. Her actual mandate from the Council is broader and less comfortable than she'd prefer.

## Notes

Sera's recruitment story mirrors the Council's institutional philosophy — find people the world has already broken, offer them purpose, and bind their loyalty through gratitude. The question the story hasn't answered yet is whether Sera recognizes this pattern or still believes her loyalty is freely given.

Her rivalry with Aldric isn't personal animosity — it's two competent people whose methods and values are incompatible, forced into proximity by the crisis. The tension is structural, not emotional, which makes it harder to resolve and more interesting to write.
