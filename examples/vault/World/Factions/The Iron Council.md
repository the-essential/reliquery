## Overview

The Iron Council styles itself as the custodian of institutional continuity — the faction that keeps the machinery of governance running while others squabble over who gets to sit in the big chair. Whether this is public service or a power play dressed in humility depends on who you ask.

## Philosophy & Structure

The Council operates through networks rather than hierarchies. Operatives are embedded across political, commercial, and cultural institutions, maintaining influence through presence rather than proclamation. The structure is deliberately opaque: most people who interact with a Council operative don't know they're interacting with a Council operative.

## History

Formed in the aftermath of the Thorngate Incident as a stabilizing force. The founders understood that the collapse happened not because of any single failure, but because no one was watching the connections between systems. The Council was designed to be that watcher.

## Key Members

- **Director Callum Vey** — Acting head since the succession crisis began. Pragmatic, exhausted, holding the institution together through force of habit.
- **Sera Voss** — Field operative embedded in Duskhollow. Intelligence and assessment.
- **Miren Cade** — Senior handler and recruiter. Sera's mentor and the architect of many current field operations.

## Current Status

Officially neutral in the succession crisis. Operationally, the Council is positioning to ensure that whoever emerges from the crisis owes them enough to preserve the Council's institutional role. This requires playing every side without being caught playing any.

## Notes

The Iron Council's core tension is that an organization designed to prevent institutional collapse is itself an institution — and therefore subject to the same pressures it was created to monitor. They watch for systemic rot everywhere except in their own structure, which makes them vulnerable to exactly the kind of slow corruption they're trained to detect in others.
