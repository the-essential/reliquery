## Overview

The Succession Crisis is the central arc of the current narrative. Three factions compete for control of Duskhollow's governance after the previous ruling body dissolved under circumstances that remain partially unexplained. The crisis is a vehicle for exploring institutional power, personal loyalty, and whether stability and justice are compatible goals.

## Current State

Negotiations in the Amber Forum have stalled. Each faction publicly advocates for a different governance model while privately maneuvering to ensure their own influence survives regardless of the outcome. Sera Voss has been embedded in Duskhollow by the Iron Council for intelligence gathering, but her actual orders extend beyond observation.

Aldric Thane has arrived as an independent negotiator — or at least, that's his stated role. His connection to the Pale Archive is suspected but unproven.

## Open Threads

The true cause of the previous government's collapse remains unresolved and may be connected to the Pale Archive's activities. Sera's handler Miren Cade has been unusually uncommunicative since Sera's deployment, which may indicate either operational security or something worse.
